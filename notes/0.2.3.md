## New
- **Designs.** `design:` in target.yaml names a target's design when it isn't the one most targets share: a phone layout, a redesign. Screenshots are compared pixel by pixel only within a design. Between designs, omnicui compares the **design language**: the colours each draws with. `palette` is the share of one's drawing in colours the other design uses in none of its screenshots at that colour scheme (default threshold 15%). Behaviour, keyboard, motion and visibility are still compared between every target. On the reference apps:
  - one design drawn by web, egui and JavaFX measures 0%;
  - a redesign in a palette of its own measures 73–100%, and the warning names its colours;
  - the same redesign in the reference colours measures 0%.
- **Parts only some designs have.** `optional: true` now also covers traces, clips and expectations. Where a target has no such part ("no part"), they are skipped with a note (`no `thumb` in webalt's design (an optional part): its trace is skipped`), and the rest of the test counts. A test whose steps act on it, or that has nothing left to check, is "not measured".
- **Looser text.** `text` and `name` take `{ contains: "3" }` or `{ matches: "^3\\b" }` (RE2) as well as an exact string.
- **Direction of motion.** Each pair of position traces is compared by where the point went. More than 30° apart is a warning (`thresholds.direction`): `web went right 20 px, webalt up 20 px`.
- `--fail-on` also takes `palette`, `direction` and `motion`.
- report.html has a "Colours across designs" table, with each palette's swatches.
- `experiments/redesign/`: a second design of the web counter and switch, run against the same contracts. It has look-only and broken-behaviour variants and contract experiments; `run.sh` re-runs it.

## Changed
- **`text` is what a part shows.** The web adapter leaves out text that only a screen reader gets (an element clipped to a 1×1 box), which it used to read as part of the value (`Count:\n5`).
- A part that a target doesn't have, and that isn't optional, fails with a message that says how to mark it optional, in addition to the per-frame lookup hint traces already gave.
