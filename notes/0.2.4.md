## New
- **Platform tags.** target.yaml says a target's forms (`platform: [mobile]`: desktop, web, mobile, tablet), and its adapter reports its OS in advertise (`platform: {os}`); `os:` in target.yaml stands in for an adapter that doesn't. Contracts select platforms by target, OS, form, design or `default`, and the most specific wins. A selector that matches nothing is a warning, with the nearest name.
- **`same:` per part.** Each part says what it holds the same between targets, per platform: `pixels`, `layout`, `colours`, `motion` or `none` (`same: { default: pixels, mobile: [layout, colours] }`). A pair is held to what both promised:
  - the whole screen is compared at the base level, with every part held to less masked;
  - those parts are compared by colour over their box, by layout, or not at all;
  - a part held to more is compared as a crop;
  - traces and clips of a part are compared only where both promised its motion.

  The base is the contract's `same` (default `pixels`) within a design, and `[colours, motion]` across designs, so a contract without `same:` compares as before.
- **Contract-level `same:` per platform:** `same: { default: pixels, mobile: none }`. A contract that says `same:` is taken at its word across designs too: `same: [layout, colours, motion]` holds a web and a macOS app of one product to one structure and palette, each drawn idiomatically.
- **Layout**, a new measure from `bounds`: each part's box relative to the component, scaled to its width. Another reading order is 100%; the threshold is `thresholds.layout`, default 10%. report.html has a "Layout across platforms" table.
- **System regions.** A screenshot reply may list the regions the platform drew (titlebar, statusbar, homeindicator, keyboard, scrollbar). They are left out of every comparison without any contract naming them (GAPS 19). A bar across the whole width at the top or bottom is cut off, so the content under a desktop title bar lines up with the web's.
- **Viewports per platform:** `viewports: { default: […], mobile: […] }`. The i-th size of each target is compared.
- **`only:` / `except:`** on a test, giving a new status, **not applicable**, counted on its own.
- **Named inputs** in `platforms.yaml`: `{ input: back }` is sent to each target as its platform's form (a key, an action, a `do:`, or `none`), and each result notes what it became.
- **Platform exceptions.** An expected value may be given per platform, with a required `why:`. Each one is listed under the result and counted in the summary.

## Fixed
- The fake adapter that omnicui's tests run against read its settings for every pixel of every frame (since 0.2.3), so a recorded clip took up to 2.8 s on a busy machine and tests timed out at 2 s. It now reads them once per frame, and a clip takes 0.17 s.
