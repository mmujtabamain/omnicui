## New
- **Run less:** `check --test <name>` (part of a name, or a glob with `*`) and `check --failed` (what failed, broke or warned last time).
- **Time:** each progress line and result has its time; the summary lists the slowest tests and each target's total and start-up time. While a target starts, a line every 10 s shows the time left and the adapter's last stderr line.
- **`exclusive: true`** in `target.yaml`, or `check --serial`: a target that needs the whole machine (an emulator, a simulator) runs with no other target alongside.
- **Masks:** `screenshot: { mask: [parts] }` on a test and `mask: [parts]` for a contract leave those parts out of both screenshots of a pair. A part can be `optional: true` (window controls a desktop toolbar has and the web one doesn't). `screenshot: { part: toolbar }` compares only that part's box on each target. The whole screenshots' measures stay in the report.
- **Actions the targets define:** `{ do: swipe, part: row, with: { direction: left } }` is passed to the target unchanged and advertised as `do.swipe`. SPEC lists scroll, swipe, refresh, back and pick as conventions, not built-ins.
- **Motion:** `trace: { part, property: scale }`; `record: { part: x }` measures a clip over one part's box; a change that goes out and comes back (a spring, a hover in and out) is measured out and back instead of "not compared".
- **`selected`** on buttons (a chip, a tab, a segment), for targets that advertise it.
- **`keys` in `advertise`:** a target can say which keys activate each role on its platform.

## Changed
- **Generated keyboard tests** are "not measured", not failed, when their part isn't on screen when the component mounts (with the advice to give a sheet or a dialog its own component), and not passed when the press changes nothing. The summary counts "not measured" when there is any.
- **Traces look their part up at each frame** (protocol and the three reference targets), so a part that appears during the steps is traced from its first frame. omnicui says so when a target can't find the part.
- **A failing test keeps its adapter's stderr** from while it ran, printed under it and in `results.json`.

## Fixed
- **report-test's `node_modules` (36 MB) is no longer in git,** so a vendored copy of `tools/omnicui` is source only.
- **Generated keyboard tests' calls** are logged under their own test and combination.
